Drive Caffeine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Short version: Drive Caffeine collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers, accounts, analytics, or tracking. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What the app does

Drive Caffeine is a macOS menu-bar utility that keeps external drives from spinning down. It periodically reads (or, optionally, writes) a small amount of data to a drive you explicitly choose, so the drive's firmware idle timer never fires. That is its only function.

Data we collect

None. Drive Caffeine does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no telemetry, no analytics SDK, and no advertising.

Network access

Drive Caffeine makes no network connections of any kind. It does not contact us or any third party. Your information cannot leave your Mac because the app never sends anything off it.

What stays on your device

To do its job, the app stores a few things locally, inside its own sandboxed container on your Mac. None of this is transmitted anywhere:

  • Drive access permissions. When you add a drive, macOS asks you to grant access via a file picker. The app saves a “security-scoped bookmark” so it can reach that same drive after a restart. This is a standard macOS mechanism and is stored only in the app's local settings.
  • Your preferences. Which drives you added, the read/write mode, and the interval — stored locally so your setup persists.
  • A keep-alive marker file. On writable drives, the app may create a small hidden file named .drivecaffeine_keepalive at the drive's root and write a few bytes to it on a timer. It never grows and contains no personal data.
  • Diagnostic logs. The app writes activity messages to the macOS unified log (Console) for troubleshooting. These stay on your Mac and are never uploaded.

Files the app reads

In Read mode, the app reads a small portion (the first 64 KB) of an existing file on the drive you selected, solely to generate disk activity. It does not inspect, copy, transmit, or store the contents of your files.

Children's privacy

Drive Caffeine is a utility with no data collection and is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children.
