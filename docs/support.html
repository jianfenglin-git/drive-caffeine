Drive Caffeine — Support

Keep your external drive awake on macOS.

Getting started

  1. Click the Drive Caffeine icon in your menu bar.
  2. Click Add Drive… and pick your external drive.
  3. Leave Read checked (the default). The drive now stays awake.

Frequently asked questions

My drive still spins down. What do I do?

  • Lower the interval. Some drives sleep faster than 30 seconds. Drag the slider down (e.g. to 10 seconds) so the keep-alive fires more often.
  • Enable Write. A few drives reset their idle timer only on writes, not reads. Check the Write box for that drive.

What's the difference between Read and Write?

Read (default) gently reads a small piece of a file on the drive — no wear, and it works even on read-only drives like Plex media shares or Time Machine volumes. Write writes a tiny hidden marker file. Use Write only if Read doesn't keep your particular drive awake.

It says “read-only — no readable file found.”

Read mode needs at least one file on the drive to read. If the drive is empty and read-only, there's nothing to read — add a file, or use a writable drive.

What is the .drivecaffeine_keepalive file on my drive?

That's the tiny hidden marker Write mode uses. It's a few bytes, never grows, and is safe to delete (the app recreates it if needed). It only appears when Write mode is enabled.

The app disappeared / there's no Dock icon.

That's by design — Drive Caffeine lives only in the menu bar, not the Dock. Look for its icon near the clock at the top-right of your screen.

Does it keep my Mac awake too?

No. It only keeps the drive awake while your Mac is on. When your Mac sleeps, Drive Caffeine pauses and lets the drive sleep too.

Does it collect any data?

No. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
